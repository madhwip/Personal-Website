About Me

I am Madhwi Pandey, a product leader and entrepreneur with over 13 years of experience across Amazon, AWS, and Wells Fargo. I build at the intersection of technology, human behaviour, and meaningful impact.

I am the founder of five ventures: Améliore Luxe (luxury fashion), UttamVeda (Vedic astrology platform), The Kayr (mental health marketplace), Saavvo (financial literacy for children), and CraftsYou (craft kits and workshops). Each venture addresses a distinct gap — in beauty, wellness, mental health, financial education, and creative fulfilment.

My work is driven by a belief that the most valuable products are the ones that quietly improve how people live — not just what they buy.

My Ventures

✦ Améliore Luxe

Ameliore Luxe is more than just a fashion brand—it's a statement of elegance and functionality. Specializing in premium raincoats and bespoke cosplay costumes, Ameliore Luxe caters to discerning customers who value both style and substance. Our products are designed to meet the highest standards of quality and craftsmanship, ensuring that every piece is as durable as it is beautiful.

With a rapidly growing customer base and a presence in key fashion markets, Ameliore Luxe is poised for significant expansion. We are committed to redefining luxury fashion by blending innovation with timeless design.

Explore more about Ameliore Luxe at amelioreluxe.com.

🔮 UttamVeda

UttamVeda is a Jyotish-powered astrology platform that delivers personalised Vedic guidance — built on the precision of exact birth time and location, not generic sun sign horoscopes.

The platform connects users with verified Jyotish practitioners for live consultations, daily transit alerts mapped to their personal chart, and structured learning modules in Vedic astrology and esoteric sciences.

Explore: uttamveda.com

⊹ The Kayr

The Kayr is India's first therapist matching marketplace — connecting individuals to the right psychologist based on multi-factor compatibility: specialisation, issue type, language, cultural background, availability, and budget.

Unlike generic therapy directories, The Kayr structures care into short-term and long-term plans, enabling both clients and practitioners to engage with intention and measurable progress.

Join the waitlist: thekayr.com

◈ Saavvo

Saavvo is a gamified financial literacy platform designed for Indian children and teenagers aged 8–18. Through chore-based earning systems, savings goal tracking, UPI simulations, and parent-child engagement tools, Saavvo builds real money habits from childhood.

India has 567 million children under 18. Most will enter adulthood without ever having been taught how money works. Saavvo is building the platform to change that.

Explore: saavvo.com

❖ CraftsYou

CraftsYou creates craft kits and workshops for adults who want to make things — without the chaos of sourcing materials or the overwhelm of starting from scratch. From macrame to resin art to clay painting, every kit is designed for real life: small windows, small spaces, and the quiet satisfaction of making something with your hands.

Explore: craftsyou.com
